Chicken Parmigiana

About

The root of all that is good in the world, a Chicken Parmigiana (or a Parmi as it's often referred to) is an Australian delicacy and one of the country's favourite meals.

Often served alongside a simple salad and on top of a pile of hot chips, it's the nation's comfort pub food.

Ingredients

  • 3 chicken breasts
  • 1tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1/2 tsp Italian mixed herbs
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups panko bread crumbs
  • 1/2 cup parmesan
  • Olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cup onion or eschallots
  • 400g tomato passata
  • 1/2 cup chicken stock/broth
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Sprinkle both sides of chicken with salt, pepper and Italian herbs. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add garlic and onion, cook for 3 minutes until onion is translucent.
  3. Add tomato, chicken stock, salt and pepper. Cover, then simmer 10 minutes on low until thickened. Cover and keep warm.
  4. Preheat oven to 180°C/350°F.
  5. Crumbing set up: Spread flour out in a shallow bowl. Whisk Egg Dredge in a separate shallow bowl. Mix Crumbing ingredients in a third shallow bowl or pan.
  6. Dredge & crumb: Press both sides of chicken into flour, then shake off excess. Coat in egg, letting excess drip off, then place in Crumbing, pressing to adhere.
  7. Transfer to plate and repeat with remaining chicken.
  8. Line a tray with paper towels and place rack on tray.
  9. Heat 1.5 to 2cm of oil in a skillet over medium high heat to 180°C/350°F.
  10. Carefully place 2 or 3 pieces of chicken in. Cook 2 minutes until underside is golden, then carefully turn and cook the other side for 1 1/2 minutes.
  11. Transfer to rack, then repeat with remaining chicken.
  12. Place chicken on a tray. Spoon 1/3 cup sauce over the chicken to cover around 80% of the chicken, leave the ends crispy.
  13. Sprinkle sauce with mozzarella cheese (pile it on in mounds to use it all up), then sprinkle with parmesan. Drizzle oil over parmesan.
  14. Bake 15 minutes until cheese is melted and there’s a few brown spots. No longer, otherwise chicken will overcook.
  15. Remove from oven and serve immediately (with leftover Tomato Sauce, if you'd like!)
